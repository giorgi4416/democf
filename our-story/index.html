Our Story

Made for slow mornings.

MORNING. is a small, independent café in Vake — built by people who believe the first hours of the day deserve real care.

01How it started

From a home roaster to a corner café.

  1. 2023

    The first roast

    Nino starts roasting green coffee on a tiny home roaster, sharing bags with friends and neighbours.

  2. 2024

    Pop-up mornings

    Weekend pop-ups at Tbilisi markets turn a hobby into a small, loyal following.

  3. 2025

    Finding the corner

    A sunlit corner in Vake becomes available — with big windows and room for a real kitchen.

  4. 2026

    Doors open

    MORNING. opens with a bakery, a brunch kitchen and our own weekly roast.

02Philosophy

Three things we believe in.

  1. 01

    Slow is a feature

    We don't rush coffee, dough or guests. Good things take the time they take.

  2. 02

    Know where it comes from

    Every bean, egg and jar of honey has a name and a place behind it.

  3. 03

    Warmth over formality

    Premium doesn't have to mean stiff. We want you to feel at home from the very first visit.

03Atmosphere

Light, wood and quiet music.

Big windows, oak tables and a long bar where you can watch your coffee being made. Mornings are calm and bright; afternoons are made for long conversations and a second slice of cake.

  • Sunlit window seats
  • A quiet corner for working
  • A dog-friendly terrace
  • Free Wi-Fi

04Ingredients

Good food starts with good partners.

We cook with what Georgia's small producers do best, and we visit them whenever we can.

  • Free-range eggs

    From a family farm in Mukhrani, delivered three times a week.

  • Vegetables & herbs

    Seasonal produce from small growers around Mtskheta.

  • Wildflower honey

    From beekeepers in Racha — in our lemonade, iced tea and yogurt.

  • Stone-milled flour

    For the sourdough and pastries we bake every night.

05The team

The people behind your morning.

Sample content for demonstration

  • Nino

    Founder & Roaster

    Roasts every batch herself and still works the Monday bar shift.

  • Mariam

    Head Barista

    Latte-art champion of our staff room and keeper of the dial-in notebook.

  • Luka

  • Ana
