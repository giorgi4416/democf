02Roasting

Roasted every Monday, tasted every morning.

Our coffee is roasted in small batches on a 5 kg drum roaster, with a profile built for each bean. Then it rests — and our baristas take over.

  1. 01

    Sourcing

    Small lots from importers who pay producers well above the market price.

  2. 02

    Roasting

    Light-to-medium roasts that keep the character of the origin.

  3. 03

    Resting

    Five to ten days of rest so the flavours settle before grinding.

  4. 04

    Dialling in

    Every morning we taste and adjust grind, dose and time.
