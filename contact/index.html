03Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do you take reservations?

Yes — by phone. Call us and we'll hold a table for you. Walk-ins are always welcome too.

Can you host a group or a private event?

We seat groups of up to 16. For 8 or more guests please call at least a day ahead; for private events, give us a call and we'll talk it through.

Do you have vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes. Our menu marks vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free dishes, and oat or almond milk is available for every coffee.

Is there Wi-Fi? Can I work from the café?

Wi-Fi is free. Laptops are welcome on weekdays; on weekend mornings we keep tables free for brunch.

Are dogs welcome?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the terrace — we'll bring a bowl of water.

Can I buy your coffee beans?

Yes — all our single origins are available in 250 g bags at the bar, ground to order.
